DATA AND SCIENTIFIC CONSTRAINTS:
- Original 44,000 legacy JPGs lack patient/augmentation-family IDs. Exclude 1,863
  rows connected to original 100 by decoded-pixel/dHash; additionally exclude 14,959
  mixed-label hash-group rows as a conservative sensitivity choice. dHash collision
  is not proof of label error or same person. Retain 27,178 images: 19,023 train,
  2,705 validation, 5,450 test. All 4 stage classes remain present in each split.
- Default stage epoch visits every retained training image once, shuffled. Loss is
  inversely weighted by hash-group size and number of class groups. It never treats
  missing patient IDs as recovered. --stage-draws is an explicit optional reduced
  stochastic budget; leave 0 for the final full-pass experiments.
- Multitask adds genuine AD/CN binary loss, weight 0.25, every 4 stage batches, using
  a separate 2-output head. Sampling balances clinical classes/subjects and chooses
  one random slice per sampled subject. Never map AD/MCI to 4 severity stages.
- Besga 54 AD/CN subjects split BEFORE 9 slices: 32 train / 11 validation / 11 test. Exclude 24
  bipolar subjects. OpenNeuro 20 entirely external: 14 CN / 1 AD / 5 MCI. MCI gets predictions,
  no AD/CN metric label. Clinical probabilities average over 9 slices per subject.
- No test-based tuning. All 100 is historical and old checkpoint exposed 66 train,
  9 validation, 25 test images. This is a descriptive reader benchmark, not independent
  clinical validation. Hash exclusion does not establish patient independence.
- Small clinical cohorts, especially only 1 external AD case, cannot substantiate
  reliable diagnostic performance or strong clinical superiority claims.
- Per-epoch train/validation losses, training/validation accuracies, source/head
  validation metrics, raw curve PNG, config and resumable checkpoints are saved.
  Curves are never smoothed or cherry-picked. Smoke curves are explicitly labeled.
- Point estimates and case-aligned predictions are prepared by the evaluator.
  Manuscript uncertainty analysis, including paired comparisons and confidence
  intervals at the appropriate image/subject unit, remains a separate prespecified
  analysis step. The 100-image reader benchmark is not patient independent.
