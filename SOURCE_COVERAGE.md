# Current source coverage

Current research tables and regenerated plots are under `results/current/`. The STS Results manuscript and reproducible historical training/full-test/supporting-benchmark audits are under `results/sts/`, with source code in `analysis/sts_results/`. The active clinical scope contains two reused cohorts and zero fresh external clinical cohorts. Historical snapshots remain in Git history. Retired exact-cohort comparison scripts and combined artifacts were removed from the active tree; the generic statistical helpers and current validated runner replace them.

The Mendeley release is recorded as an alias of already-present image content, not additional training data. OASIS intake is implemented but actual access, acquisition, preprocessing and evaluation remain pending. Private clinical images, participant records, access receipts, credentials and operational material are not redistributed. Existing historical dementia-stage evidence and trained checkpoints remain included.

The six Round 1 runs completed 305 epochs. Their 5,450-image replay remains a post-hoc descriptive supplement containing the original 1,058-image primary comparison and nested 756-image sensitivity subset. Source clinical endpoints and the reused 100-image benchmark retain their separate interpretations. The combined [six-page STS manuscript](results/sts/README.md) contains four tables and four embedded graphs, keeping those historical results separate from the new experiment.

The six October 18 retraining runs completed **329 epochs**. The [report](results/sts/retraining/report.json) and [public run evidence](results/sts/retraining/raw/) include full histories, aggregate per-epoch evaluations, validation selection, curves and source hashes. Clean training and validation NLL were measured at the same epoch-end weights with the same deterministic transform, evaluation mode and unweighted unsmoothed loss. Online training loss and the weighted smoothed optimization objective remain separate fields. These runs add no held-out or external clinical performance estimates.

All twelve original retraining best/last checkpoint hashes and the private clinical development manifest were verified locally for the publication report. The six public [inference-state derivatives](models/sts_retrain_20261018/README.md) have distinct file hashes; their [provenance](models/sts_retrain_20261018/provenance.json) identifies the selected originals and records unchanged tensor bytes and safe-loading checks. Original resumable checkpoints and private clinical manifests are not redistributed. A public rebuild from saved metadata verifies the histories and available manifest evidence, while explicitly marking absent originals and the private clinical manifest as metadata-only checks.

The repository manifest includes new public code and outputs, excludes private/raw-data working bundles, and uses payload SHA256/size for LFS files. Local pointer identities do not constitute an independent rehash of unavailable model binaries.
